FAQS

 
 

HOW DO I PLACE AN ORDER USING FLEEK CREDIT?

Choose Fleek Credit as your payment method at checkout on the app. You’ll be redirected to verify your identity with our credit partner. That’s it! Your order will be placed using your credit limit.

 

HOW DO I CHECK MY LIMIT?

You can check how much credit you have available in your account page on the app. You can place as many orders as you like using your available limit. Every time you repay, your limit goes back up.

 

WHAT DO I DO WHEN IT'S TIME TO REPAY?

You will have 45 days after you place your order to repay. You will receive an email and SMS from our credit partner (Two) five days before it's time to repay, and a second reminder on your due date. You can repay via bank transfer and the account details will be provided on the invoice.
